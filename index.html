概要

『Desktop Forge』は、作業中のデスクトップの片隅に置いたまま進行する、放置型の制作・経営シミュレーションです。

工房の職人には、能力だけでなく190種類以上の個性と、得意分野、好き嫌いがあります。プレイヤーは一人ずつ指示を出すのではなく、任せる仕事を決めます。職人たちは自分で採集・加工・鍛造・休憩を選んで動き、誰をどの工程に置くかが、工房の流れと完成する剣を変えていきます。

投入した素材、担当した職人、そのときの感情や作業の出来によって、剣は一本ごとに切れ味・耐久力・エンチャントの配分と品質が変わります。打ち上がったばかりの剣は真価が分からず、鑑定を進めることで段階的に明らかになります。

鑑定を終えた剣は、工房を訪れた冒険者へ託します。冒険者は工房の従業員ではありません。育てることも、呼び戻すこともできず、プレイヤーが関与できるのは彼らが持つ剣だけです。冒険者は剣を受け取る前に行き先を告げ、行き先ごとに求められる剣の形は異なります。倉庫のどの一本を持たせるかが、あなたの下す唯一の判断です。

噛み合った剣は冒険者を最後まで運び、報酬と名声を積んだログとして返ってきます。足りない剣は途中で砕け、持ち主は傷を負って引き返します。そして力の足りない剣を持たせた冒険者は、二度と工房へ戻りません。その顛末は工房の年表に刻まれ、消えることはありません。

About

Desktop Forge is an idle simulation that runs in a small window at the edge of your desktop while you work.

Every artisan has more than a set of stats: over 190 traits, plus their own specialties, likes and dislikes. You never direct them move by move — you decide which jobs each of them is allowed to take, and they choose when to gather, process, forge or rest. Who you put on which stage shapes the flow of the workshop and the swords that come out of it.

The materials used, the artisans involved, their moods and the quality of their work shape how each sword splits between edge, durability and enchantment, and how well it is made. A newly forged blade hides its true nature; appraisal uncovers it step by step.

Appraised swords go to the adventurers who visit your workshop. They are not your employees — you cannot train them, command them, or bring them home. The only thing you control is the sword in their hands. Each one announces where they are headed before taking a blade, and every destination asks for a different shape of sword. Which one you take out of storage is the only call you make.

A well-matched sword carries its bearer through the whole route and comes back as a log full of coin and renown. A poor one breaks partway, and its bearer limps home wounded. And an adventurer sent out with a blade that isn't good enough never returns at all. What happened is written into your workshop's chronicle, and it stays there.

Key features

  • A desktop companion — a compact workshop that keeps moving in the corner of your screen
  • Autonomous artisans — set work policies and watch an idle workshop run on its own
  • Over 190 traits — specialties, likes, dislikes and moods that shape the work
  • A different sword every time — materials, artisans, emotions and luck shape its stats and quality
  • Arm an adventurer — whether your blade answers what the road demands decides the outcome
  • Lives on the line — a weak blade breaks, its bearer never returns, and only the record remains
  • Long-term goals — codex records, a workshop expansion tree, and Steam leaderboards
