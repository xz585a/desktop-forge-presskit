ABOUT
================================================================

Desktop Forge is an idle simulation that runs in a small window at the edge of your desktop
while you work.

Every artisan has more than a set of stats: over 190 traits, plus their own specialties,
likes and dislikes. You never direct them move by move — you decide which jobs each of them
is allowed to take, and they choose when to gather, process, forge or rest. Who you put on
which stage shapes the flow of the workshop and the swords that come out of it.

The materials used, the artisans involved, their moods and the quality of their work shape
how each sword splits between edge, durability and enchantment, and how well it is made. A
newly forged blade hides its true nature; appraisal uncovers it step by step.

Appraised swords go to the adventurers who visit your workshop. They are not your employees
— you cannot train them, command them, or bring them home. The only thing you control is the
sword in their hands. Each one announces where they are headed before taking a blade, and
every destination asks for a different shape of sword. Which one you take out of storage is
the only call you make.

A well-matched sword carries its bearer through the whole route and comes back as a log full
of coin and renown. A poor one breaks partway, and its bearer limps home wounded. And an
adventurer sent out with a blade that isn't good enough never returns at all. What happened
is written into your workshop's chronicle, and it stays there.

================================================================
KEY FEATURES
================================================================

- A desktop companion — a compact workshop that keeps moving in the corner of your screen
- Autonomous artisans — set work policies and watch an idle workshop run on its own
- Over 190 traits — specialties, likes, dislikes and moods that shape the work
- A different sword every time — materials, artisans, emotions and luck shape its stats and quality
- Arm an adventurer — whether your blade answers what the road demands decides the outcome
- Lives on the line — a weak blade breaks, its bearer never returns, and only the record remains
- Long-term goals — codex records, a workshop expansion tree, and Steam leaderboards

DEVELOPER
================================================================

Nulpoyo. A solo developer based in Japan, making games alongside a full-time office job.

I like games with a lot to chew on — which means mine tend to end up harder than I
intended. Most of the work on Desktop Forge has gone into balancing: keeping it something
you can leave running at the edge of your screen, while still leaving numbers worth
pushing on.
